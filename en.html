Deep learning, made simple.

We’ve packaged a full Python 3.12 environment, PyTorch, and the official HTDemucs model into a single app. There’s no terminal, no setup — just open DeepStem Pro and start separating stems.

The model processes stereo audio natively, preserving the original warmth and depth. It handles CD‑quality and high‑resolution files up to 48 kHz, giving you vocal and instrumental tracks that sound exactly like the source.
