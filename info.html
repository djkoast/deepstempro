Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AIFF. Drag & drop or use the file picker.

Is my audio uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs offline on your Mac. No network access is used.

Why does separation take a while?

The app uses a built-in neural network model. Longer files take longer to process. A progress bar shows how far it is.

How do I export the results?

After separation, use the Export buttons to save WAV or AIFF files.

Can I cancel processing?

Yes — click the Cancel button while processing is running.
